02 / Research approach

Understand. Intervene.
Build on what holds.

I connect mechanistic analysis with changes to architecture and training. Each intervention is a way to test an explanation.

I.

Read the representation

Use phase, Fourier structure, geometry, and causal probing to investigate how information is encoded and used.

Features · Circuits · Symmetries
II.

Test the mechanism

Change internal variables, architectural constraints, or training data. Measure which predictions survive the intervention.

Causal interventions · Training dynamics
III.

Turn findings into design

Translate supported mechanisms into phase-preserving architectures, geometric inductive biases, and optical computation.

Architectures · Inductive biases · Optics
Currently exploring

How training data shapes the internal algorithms that language models learn.
